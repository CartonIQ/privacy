3. Developer & Infrastructure

CartonIQ applications are built with an offline-first architecture.

  • We do not operate our own backend servers.
  • We do not use Firebase or Supabase.
  • We do not maintain a cloud database for user content.
  • We do not require mandatory user accounts or login systems.

We do not have access to user-created content that remains stored locally on your device.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

To keep our applications free, we integrate Google AdMob. We use Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded advertisements. We do not intentionally use Native or App Open advertisements.

Data Collection by AdMob

While our apps do not send your personal content to our servers, Google AdMob collects data to serve advertisements. The SDK may collect or receive: IP address, Android Advertising ID, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

For more information, review Google's official documentation: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Data Disclosure.

Consent & Personalized Ads

We use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. Personalized advertising is supported subject to your consent. You can manage your advertising consent choices at any time through the Settings area of our applications.

5. Local Data Storage

Our applications perform their main functions locally on your device. Depending on the app, the following information may be stored locally: calculations, notes, records, pet information, habit progress, recipes, game progress, and generated documents/audio files.

CartonIQ does not routinely access information that remains exclusively in an app's private local storage.

6. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell your locally stored application content. Information may only be shared with: 1) Google AdMob for advertising and security, 2) Other apps when you explicitly choose to export or share a file, and 3) Legal authorities if required by law.

Data Deletion

Local app data remains until you delete it in-app, clear the app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Note that uninstalling an app does not delete files you have exported to shared storage (like your Downloads folder).

7. Permissions

  • Microphone/Audio (Echo, VoxMorph): For audio amplification, voice effects, and TTS.
  • Camera (ScanGenius, Echo): For QR/barcode scanning and flashlight control.
  • Files/Media (PDF Studio, BuildIQ, CartonIQ, AgeWish, VoxMorph): For opening, exporting, and saving files.

Our current designs do not intentionally request access to your Location, Contacts, Phone, SMS, or Call Logs.

8. Children's Privacy

The applications listed in this policy are not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not intentionally target children under 13. We set accurate target-audience settings in the Google Play Console for every application.

9. Security

We design our applications to keep ordinary user-created content locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You should protect your device and any files you export. CartonIQ cannot guarantee absolute security.
