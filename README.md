## 3. Developer and Infrastructure

CartonIQ applications are built with an **offline-first** architecture. 
*   We do not operate our own backend servers.
*   We do not use Firebase.
*   We do not use Supabase.
*   We do not maintain a cloud database for user content.
*   We do not require mandatory user accounts or login systems.

We do not have access to user-created content that remains stored locally on your device.

---

## 4. Advertising — Google AdMob

To keep our applications free, we integrate **Google AdMob**. 

**Ad Formats Used:**
*   Banner advertisements
*   Interstitial advertisements
*   Rewarded advertisements

We do not intentionally use Native advertisements or App Open advertisements.

**Data Collection by Google AdMob:**
While our apps do not send your personal content to our servers, Google AdMob collects data to serve advertisements. According to Google's policies, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive:
*   IP address and network information.
*   Android Advertising ID or device identifiers.
*   Advertising interactions (impressions, clicks).
*   Diagnostic information for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and security.

The exact information depends on your device settings, your consent choices, and Google's services. For more information, please review Google's official documentation:
*   Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
*   AdMob Data Disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

**Consent and Personalized Advertising:**
We use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. Personalized advertising is supported subject to your consent, regional requirements, and Google's configuration. You can manage your advertising consent choices at any time through the Settings area of our applications.

## 5. Local Data Storage

Our applications are designed to perform their main functions locally on your device. Depending on the app, the following information may be stored locally in the app's private storage:
*   Calculations and preferences
*   Notes and records
*   Pet information and subscription records
*   Habit progress and vocabulary progress
*   Recipes and kitchen information
*   Game progress
*   Generated documents and files (e.g., PDFs, audio files)

CartonIQ does not routinely access information that remains exclusively in an app's private local storage.

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Retention

**We do not sell your locally stored application content.**

Information may only be shared in these specific circumstances:
1.  **Google AdMob:** For advertising, measurement, consent, and security purposes.
2.  **User-Initiated Actions:** When you explicitly export, print, save, or share files using Android features or another application.
3.  **Legal Requirements:** When disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

**Data Retention and Deletion:**
Local app data generally remains on your device until you:
*   Delete it using an in-app feature (where available).
*   Clear the application storage via Android settings.
*   Uninstall the application.

*Important:* Uninstalling an app removes its private data, but exported files in shared storage (like your Downloads folder) or files shared with other apps may remain on your device. Google handles advertising-related information under its own retention policies.

## 10. Security

We design our applications to keep ordinary user-created content locally on your device. We do not routinely access private local app data. 

However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You should protect your device and any files you export. Advertising-related communication is handled by Google under its own security practices. CartonIQ cannot guarantee absolute security.
